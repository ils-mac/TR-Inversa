# Entry of U.S. Military Personnel into Mexico

## Document 1 - Letter

[SEAL: UNITED MEXICAN STATES – PRESIDENCY OF THE REPUBLIC]

JOSÉ GERARDO RODOLFO FERNÁNDEZ NOROÑA<br> 
PRESIDENT OF THE GOVERNING BOARD<br>
OF THE SENATE<br>
OF THE HONORABLE CONGRESS OF THE UNION<br>

Article 76, section III, of the Political Constitution of the United Mexican States provides that it is the exclusive authority of the Senate of the Republic to authorize the Federal Executive to allow the transit of foreign troops through national territory.

The Ministry of the Navy (SEMAR) received an invitation from the Defense Coordination Office at the Embassy of the United States of America to conduct the training program entitled **“Strengthening the Operational Capacity of the SEMAR Special Operations Forces,”** which, if approved, will be held at the Specialized Naval Infantry Training and Instruction Center, located in San Luis Carpizo, Campeche, from February 17 to March 28, 2025.

The purpose of the program is to enable personnel of the U.S. Army Special Operations Command and personnel of the Special Operations Unit of SEMAR to carry out training related to the application of tactics, techniques, and procedures for conventional and unconventional warfare in amphibious operations across maritime, land, and air environments.

Subject to authorization by the Senate, a delegation composed of ten members of the 7th Special Forces Group of the U.S. Army is to enter national territory, accompanied by the weapons, ammunition, materials, and specialized equipment necessary for combined training.

The instructors will arrive at Campeche International Airport aboard a C-130 military aircraft of the U.S. Army on February 15, 2025, and will depart the country on March 30, 2025.

In light of the foregoing, pursuant to Article 76, section III, of the Political Constitution of the United Mexican States, I hereby submit for consideration by the Senate of the Honorable Congress of the Union a request for authorization for the entry into national territory of military personnel of the U.S. Army to participate in the training program entitled “Strengthening the Operational Capacity of the SEMAR Special Operations Forces,” to be held at the Specialized Naval Infantry Training and Instruction Center, located in San Luis Carpizo, Campeche, from February 17 to March 28, 2025.

**DECREE whereby authorization is granted to the President of the United Mexican States to allow the entry into national territory of ten members of the U.S. Army, for the purpose of participating in the training program entitled “Strengthening the Operational Capacity of the SEMAR Special Operations Forces,” to be held at the Specialized Naval Infantry Training and Instruction Center, located in San Luis Carpizo, Campeche, from February 17 to March 28, 2025.**

**CLAUDIA SHEINBAUM PARDO**, President of the United Mexican States, to its inhabitants, be it known:

That the Senate of the Honorable Congress of the Union has seen fit to address to me the following

**DECREE**

“THE SENATE OF THE HONORABLE CONGRESS OF THE UNION, IN EXERCISE OF THE AUTHORITY CONFERRED UPON IT BY ARTICLE 76, SECTION III, OF THE POLITICAL CONSTITUTION OF THE UNITED MEXICAN STATES,

**DECREES:**

**1.** The Senate of the Honorable Congress of the Union, in exercise of the authority conferred upon it by Article 76, section III, of the Political Constitution of the United Mexican States, grants authorization to the President of the United Mexican States to allow the entry into national territory of ten members of the U.S. Army, for the purpose of participating in the training program entitled "**Strengthening the Operational Capacity of the SEMAR Special Operations Forces**," to be held at the Specialized Naval Infantry Training and Instruction Center, located in San Luis Carpizo, Campeche, from February 17 to March 28, 2025.

**2.** The Federal Executive is respectfully requested to direct the Secretary of the Navy to submit to this Sovereign body a report on the results of the training program.

**Transitional Provisions**

**1.** This Decree shall be in full force and effect upon its approval by the Senate.

**2.** It shall be published in the Official Gazette of the Federation.

In accordance with Article 89, section I, of the Political Constitution of the United Mexican States, I hereby issue this Decree at the residence of the Federal Executive Branch, in Mexico City, on February 13, 2025, for its publication and compliance. - **Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo**, President of the United Mexican States.- Signature.- **Rosa Icela Rodríguez Velázquez**, Secretary of the Interior. - Signature.

### INFORMADOR.MX | BREAKING NEWS

### Senate Unanimously Approves Entry of U.S. Marines into Mexico to Train Ministry of the Navy (SEMAR) Personnel

**The Senate approved the request for the entry of ten members of the U.S. Army**

By: SUN.

February 11, 2025 - 4:02 PM

![Navy personnel detain individuals with their hands raised during an operation in a forested area](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/alainamb/uic_tr18-trad-inversa-es-en/main/unidad4/semana11/imagenes/Sun_noticia1-image.png)

By unanimous vote, President Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo secured *authorization from the Senate plenary to allow the entry into national territory of ten members of the U.S. Army*,  for the purpose of participating in the training program entitled “Strengthening the Operational Capacity of the SEMAR Special Operations Forces.”

This training will take place from *February 17 to March 28* at the Specialized Naval Infantry Training and Instruction Center, located in San Luis Carpizo, Campeche.

Senator Lili Téllez, from the National Action Party (PAN), stated before the Senate that this type of international activity “reminds us how cooperation between both military forces (the United States and Mexico) is indeed possible.”

"U.S. and Mexican forces cooperating do not constitute an invasion of Mexico. There is no violation of sovereignty, the nation has not been sold out, and the country has not been subjugated simply because this cooperation occurs regularly."

She stated that, this being the case, Mexico should accept cooperation with the United States to act against cartels—after all, why shouldn’t it? She then affirmed that such cooperation should take place under a legal framework such as the one currently authorized, as has been the case in similar exercises over the years.

She asserted that a collaboration with the United States against the cartels **would not violate Mexico’s sovereignty** and could also save the lives of Mexicans “whom Morena has left defenseless in the war waged by organized crime against citizens. This shows that it is indeed clear that cooperation is possible without subjugation,” she insisted.

#### Mexico cannot defeat cartels alone

Lili Téllez stated that *Mexico lacks sufficient capacity to effectively confront criminal organizations on its own*,  otherwise it would have already done so.

When the decree was introduced, Senator Rolando Zapata Bello of the Institutional Revolutionary Party (PRI), Secretary of the Navy Commission, affirmed that cooperation with the U.S. in training Navy personnel represents a significant opportunity to strengthen national security.

He stated that the proposal had been carefully analyzed **to ensure that it preserves national sovereignty while enhancing domestic capabilities**.

“Combined training with the United States Armed Forces can strengthen our combat, rescue, navigation, and tactical equipment handling capabilities, boosting the professionalism and efficiency of our Navy to respond more effectively to the growing threats facing the country,” argued the former governor of Yucatán.

## Document 4 - 2nd News Item

### El Heraldo de Saltillo | MEXICO

### The Entry of U.S. Military Personnel to Train Navy Personnel Has Now Been Officially Approved

By Agencias - February 17, 2025

![Military personnel secure an area with official vehicles while forming a line during a security operation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/alainamb/uic_tr18-trad-inversa-es-en/main/unidad4/semana11/imagenes/Agencias_noticia2-image.png)

<small>The Navy joins the Holy Week operation.</small>

**Mexico City.-**  On February 17, a decree was published in the Official Gazette of the Federation (DOF) authorizing the entry of U.S. military personnel into Mexico to train special operations members of the Ministry of the Navy (SEMAR).

According to the document, their presence in Mexico is intended to allow them to participate in the training exercise entitled “Strengthening the Operational Capacity of the SEMAR Special Operations Forces,” which will take place at the Specialized Naval Infantry Training and Instruction Center, in San Luis Carpizo, Campeche, from February 17 to March 28, 2025.

The decree was approved by the Senate in exercise of the authority conferred upon it by Article 76, Section III, of the Political Constitution of the United Mexican States, and was subsequently promulgated by President Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo.

The document also instructs the Ministry of the Navy to submit a report to the Senate on the results of the exercise once the training has concluded.

This authorization is in accordance with the procedures established by the Constitution and current legislation, which require Senate approval to allow the temporary presence of foreign military forces in the country. The training activity stems from security and defense cooperation agreements between Mexico and the United States.

The authorization for the entry of U.S. military personnel into Mexico comes at a time when the bilateral security relationship is shaped by two key factors: the presence of U.S. surveillance flights near Mexican territory, as well as efforts in Washington to designate several Mexican cartels as Foreign Terrorist Organizations (FTOs).

The U.S. Department of State is moving forward with the process of designating several Mexican cartels as Foreign Terrorist Organizations, a measure that would strengthen sanctions against these groups and expand the operational capacity of U.S. security agencies.

The New York Times reported that the list would include six Mexican cartels:

- Sinaloa Cartel (CDS)
- Jalisco New Generation Cartel (CJNG)
- Northeast cartel
- La Familia Michoacana
- United Cartels
- Gulf cartel (CDG)

The measure is based on an executive order signed by President Donald Trump on January 20, which seeks to classify these groups under the same category as Al-Qaeda or the Islamic State (ISIS). This designation would allow the United States to freeze their assets, cut off their funding, and expand the jurisdiction of its security agencies in the fight against transnational organized crime. (El Heraldo).
